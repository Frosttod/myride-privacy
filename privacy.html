Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  MyRIDE v1.6+

Privacy by Design

1. Who we are

MyRIDE is developed by RAIDO Development (Przemysław Rolnik). This Privacy Policy explains how the MyRIDE mobile application handles your data.

2. Data collected

MyRIDE collects and stores the following data exclusively on your device:

  • GPS location — used in real time to calculate speed, distance and route while tracking is active
  • Vehicle profiles (type, brand, model, year, mileage)
  • Recorded routes (GPS points, speed, distance, duration)
  • Fuel log entries (litres, cost, odometer reading, fuel type)
  • App settings (language, units, theme)

All data is stored locally using Hive (an embedded database). No data ever leaves your device.

3. How we use your data

  • GPS location is processed in real time on your device to compute speed and distance. It is never stored in raw form beyond the current session route points.
  • Vehicle and route data is used exclusively to display statistics within the app.
  • No data is used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than the app's core functionality.

4. Data sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or transmit your data to any third party. There are no analytics services, advertising SDKs or tracking libraries in the app.

5. Data export

You can export your data as a JSON file at any time via Settings → Export data. This file is saved to your device and is never uploaded automatically anywhere.

6. Data retention & deletion

Data is retained on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual routes in route history
  • Clear all route history via Settings → Clear route history
  • Remove all app data by uninstalling the app

7. Permissions

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — required to determine GPS position while tracking
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION — required to keep GPS active when the app is minimised (background tracking)
  • WAKE_LOCK — optional; keeps the screen on during an active session if enabled by the user
  • INTERNET — used only to check for app updates and to load map tiles in route history

8. Children's privacy

MyRIDE is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
przemyslaw.rolnik1985@gmail.com
